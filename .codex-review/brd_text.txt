Introduction
Purpose. This BRD defines the functional and business requirements for an enterprise LMS designed to streamline learning, centralize organizational knowledge, and support employee development and HR-led learning programs.
Business Objectives. The LMS shall provide an end-to-end flow from content creation and publishing to learning delivery, quiz assessment, certification, XP tracking, and program management.
In Scope. Employee learning portal, course catalog, course authoring, content delivery, quizzes, certifications, XP, learning programs, feedback, reporting, notifications, role-based administration, TNA integration, training calendar, and effectiveness measurement.
2. Scope and Stakeholders
Primary stakeholders: CEO, HR, system administrators, course owners, managers, and employees.
Primary user groups: Admin, HR user, course creator / content owner, manager, learner / employee.
The LMS shall support both mandatory learning and optional self-service learning.
Key Terms
Term
Definition
XP
Experience points awarded to employees for learning activities.
Learning Program
A structured learning path managed by HR for a target audience such as new employees or trainees.
Course Attendee
An employee assigned to or enrolled in a course.
Certification
A credential issued after a learner meets the configured course requirements.
Chapter
A logical content grouping inside a course.
TNA
Training Needs Analysis – a process to identify skill gaps and training requirements.
Learning Program Type
A category of learning program, e.g., Onboarding, Leadership Development, Technical Certification, Compliance Training, etc.
Work Unit
A department, team, or functional group that can initiate a TNA request.

Core Business Rules
All learning activities and XP transactions must be auditable with timestamp and user ID.
Certification expiry and renewal rules, where configured, shall be enforced automatically.
Course content versioning shall preserve historical learning records without altering past completions.
TNA data shall be retained for trend analysis and annual planning, with full traceability of every request (free/paid) and its approval chain.
Pre/post effectiveness data shall be stored per learner per course and aggregated anonymously for reporting.
Learning program types shall be extensible; new types can be added by Admin without code changes.
